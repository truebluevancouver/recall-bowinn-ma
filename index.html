Why recall?

Local issues deserve local attention.

The campaign has identified affordability, housing and infrastructure, traffic, transit, public safety, and government accountability as core concerns.

Affordability

Focus on housing costs and everyday affordability across the North Shore.

Traffic & infrastructure

Representation

The campaign argues constituency concerns should be at the centre of provincial representation.
