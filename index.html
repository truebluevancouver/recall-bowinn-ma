Our riding

The North Vancouver–Lonsdale Riding

Our community is a vibrant collection of neighbourhoods in the heart of the North Shore. Campaign materials estimate the riding at approximately 42,000 registered voters and describe it as spanning several residential and commercial hubs.

Neighbourhoods included

  • Lower Lonsdale & Central Lonsdale
  • Grand Boulevard & Moodyville
  • Norgate & Mosquito Creek
  • Keith Lynn
  • Most of Capilano 5 Reserve (X̱wemelch'stn)
  • Mission 1 Reserve (Eslha7an)
Why recall?

A Movement for Local Representation

The following points present the campaign’s case for recall and its criticisms of Bowinn Ma’s representation and provincial policy.

I

Affordability & Cost of Living

The campaign argues that housing prices, taxes, wages and the cost of daily essentials have made life increasingly difficult for working families, students and seniors on the North Shore.

II

Housing, Traffic & Infrastructure

The campaign opposes provincial housing mandates that it says have not been matched with adequate funding and infrastructure for schools, hospitals and transit.

III

Traffic & Transportation

The campaign points to persistent congestion at the Ironworkers and Lions Gate crossings and argues that meaningful long-term solutions have not been delivered.

IV

Public Transit

The campaign says North Shore transit remains overcrowded and under-resourced despite population growth, and calls for more reliable and substantial rapid-transit investment.

V

Public Safety & Community Wellbeing

The campaign raises concerns about street-level disorder, emergency access during gridlock, and delays and cost overruns associated with the North Shore Wastewater Treatment Plant.

VI

Government Accountability & Representation

The campaign argues that an MLA should place constituency concerns ahead of party priorities and says local voices have too often been sidelined.

VII

Provincial Policy Concerns

The campaign criticizes Bill 15, the Infrastructure Projects Act, over concerns about cabinet powers, municipal zoning, environmental rules and community consultation.

Your Signature, Your Voice

Voting record & campaign concerns

Bowinn Ma’s Record

These are the principal voting-record and performance concerns identified by the campaign. They are presented as the campaign’s interpretation of the public record.

1. Bill 15

The campaign criticizes Bowinn Ma’s role in advancing and voting for the Infrastructure Projects Act, arguing that the legislation gives the provincial government excessive power to override municipal processes and local decision-making.

2. 2019 DRIPA Vote

The campaign criticizes Ma’s support for the 2019 Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples Act and argues she did not sufficiently challenge or scrutinize the legislation on behalf of constituents.

3. “Uninvited Guests” Rhetoric

The campaign objects to rhetoric it says characterizes British Columbians as “uninvited guests,” arguing that elected representatives should use language that brings constituents together rather than divides them.

4. Carihi Secondary Infrastructure

The campaign points to delays following the Carihi Secondary fire and subsequent student advocacy at the legislature as evidence of inadequate infrastructure response under the ministry.

5. North Shore Bridge Gridlock

The campaign argues that North Shore residents continue to face severe bridge congestion and calls for substantially stronger action, including consideration of a third crossing and other major transportation solutions.
