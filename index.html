Why recall?

A movement for local representation.

The campaign’s case centres on affordability, housing and infrastructure, transportation, public safety, provincial policy, and accountability.

Affordability

The campaign argues North Shore residents face mounting pressure from housing costs and everyday expenses.

Housing & infrastructure

The campaign opposes provincial housing mandates it believes are not matched by sufficient local infrastructure and services.

Traffic & transit

Persistent congestion and the need for stronger transit capacity are central campaign concerns.

Accountability

The campaign’s central argument is that the riding needs stronger constituency-first representation.

About the recall

What recall means.

Recall is a formal democratic process in British Columbia. The campaign is organizing in North Vancouver–Lonsdale to seek accountability from the riding’s MLA.

Official rules matter

Binding petition rules, timelines, eligibility, financing, and canvasser requirements should be confirmed with Elections BC.
